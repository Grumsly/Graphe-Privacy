# GRAPHE Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026

GRAPHE is a Bible reading and study app provided by Graphe. This notice explains how information is handled in the first Google Play release, which does not offer account sign-in or cloud backup.

For privacy questions or requests, email [grapheScriptureapp@gmail.com](mailto:grapheScriptureapp@gmail.com).

## Information stored on your device

GRAPHE stores your reading preferences, saved reading position, verse notes, studies, journal entries, study links and entry revision history on your device. It also stores bundled Scripture, downloaded Bible editions and cached online Scripture and translation information. This storage lets you read offline, keep your work and return to it later.

Your notes, studies and journals may contain religious beliefs or other personal information you choose to write. GRAPHE does not automatically send this personal study content to Graphe or YouVersion. You do not need an account to read or keep local studies.

GRAPHE may keep local recovery copies of your personal database when upgrading its storage format. These copies help protect your work if an upgrade fails.

## Online Bible content

GRAPHE uses YouVersion's Bible service to obtain the available translation catalogue, translation and publisher information, online chapters and Bible downloads. When an internet connection is available, the app may refresh the catalogue automatically. Reading an online edition can also fetch nearby chapters to make navigation smoother.

These HTTPS requests contain GRAPHE's application key, the requested resource, such as an edition, book or chapter, and an English language preference. The application key identifies GRAPHE; it is not a personal account identifier. YouVersion also receives connection information, including your IP address, and can observe which resources are requested. GRAPHE does not attach your notes, studies, journals or a Google account identity to these requests.

YouVersion handles information received by its service under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.bible.com/privacy), which covers its use, retention and processing of information, including processing in the United States. Its policy covers several YouVersion products; GRAPHE uses its Bible API without YouVersion account sign-in.

Bundled and fully downloaded Bible text can be read without an internet connection. To prevent online requests while using GRAPHE, disable the device's internet connection. Features that need online content will then be unavailable.

## Troubleshooting information

GRAPHE keeps up to 50 recent diagnostic events locally. These include error details, the affected feature, timestamps and support reference codes. The app also keeps temporary network-request and cache-use counters in memory for troubleshooting. These counters are not a remote analytics service.

Diagnostic reports are not automatically uploaded to Graphe. If you choose **Share error report** in Settings, Android's sharing menu lets you choose an app and recipient. That app will handle the report under its own privacy practices. Review the report before sending it, because error details may include information about the operation that failed.

## Contacting Graphe

If you email Graphe, we receive your email address and anything you include in the message or attachments, such as an error report. We use this information to respond to your enquiry, investigate the issue and handle privacy requests. Our email provider also processes the correspondence to deliver and store email.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us by contacting the email address above. We cannot access or delete information stored only on your device remotely.

## Advertising, analytics and backup

This release has no advertising or remote analytics integration. Graphe does not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

This release has no GRAPHE account, Google sign-in or Google Drive backup. Android's automatic app backup is disabled for GRAPHE. Local recovery copies are stored on the same device and are not a cloud backup.

If you open a publisher, attribution or other external website link, your browser and the website handle that visit under their own privacy practices.

## Keeping and deleting information

Personal study information stays on your device until you remove it or clear the app's storage. You can delete studies and entries using the app's controls. To remove a verse note, clear its text and save it. Entry revision history and local recovery copies may retain earlier content, so deleting or editing an individual item does not necessarily remove every local copy.

For a complete reset of GRAPHE's local data, use Android **Settings → Apps → GRAPHE → Storage → Clear storage** (the wording may vary by device), or uninstall the app. This removes local notes, studies, preferences, diagnostic records, recovery copies, cached content and downloaded editions. Clearing storage is irreversible, and this release cannot restore your work from a cloud backup.

In GRAPHE Settings, **Clear temporary storage** removes cached online Scripture and translation information. It does not delete personal studies or explicitly downloaded editions. Downloaded editions can be removed separately through the translation library. Older cached items are also removed automatically to manage storage space.

Deleting local app data does not delete reports you have shared, email you have sent, or records held by YouVersion or another service. Contact the relevant recipient or service about those records.

## Security

GRAPHE uses Android's app storage protections for local files and HTTPS for its Bible service requests. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software up to date. GRAPHE does not add a separate password or encryption layer to its local study database.

## Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when GRAPHE's information-handling practices change and show the new date above. Any future account or cloud-backup feature will need an updated notice describing that feature before it is released.
